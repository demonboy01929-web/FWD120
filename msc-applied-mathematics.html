MSc Applied Mathematics

Explore detailed academic structure, eligibility requirements, and application procedures for MSc Applied Mathematics at South Asian University.

Programme Overview

The MSc Applied Mathematics programme offered by the Department of Mathematics provides an interdisciplinary and internationally recognized curriculum designed to foster critical thinking, advanced research capabilities, and specialized skills suited for global opportunities.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Relevant degree or qualifying high school diploma from a recognized institution with minimum required aggregate marks.
  • Valid score in South Asian University Entrance Examination or designated national/international selection channel.
  • Fulfillment of language proficiency requirements and prerequisite domain knowledge.

Selection & Admission Procedure

Candidates are evaluated based on performance in the university entrance test conducted across regional centers, followed by merit verification, interview (if applicable), and document validation.

Curriculum & Structure

Core Courses Foundational modules establishing core conceptual mastery and theoretical frameworks.
Electives & Specializations Advanced tracks permitting focused study in emerging domain areas and applied tools.
Dissertation / Project Work Guided independent research, industrial exposure, or capstone presentation.
