SOUTH ASIAN UNIVERSITY

A university built for South Asia.

South Asian University is an international university established by the eight SAARC member states. Located in New Delhi, SAU brings students and faculty together from across South Asia.

The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes with education primarily conducted in English.

NEED HELP?

Frequently asked questions

How do I apply?

Applications are submitted online. Check the programme requirements first and complete the application form for the programme you want to apply for.

How much is the application fee?

The registration fee is USD 20 per application.

What entrance tests are accepted?

The required test depends on the programme. Depending on the programme, national tests may include JEE Main, CUET, GATE, CLAT or NET/JRF.

Where can I find admission updates?

All important admission updates are available on the Notifications page.
