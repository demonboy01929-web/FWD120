Admission to Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD programmes is offered through structured alternative admission pathways.

01

SAU Entrance Test / CET Mode

Admission through specialized entrance examinations and computer-based tests conducted directly by South Asian University.

  • For other SAARC candidates: Remote proctored online tests.
